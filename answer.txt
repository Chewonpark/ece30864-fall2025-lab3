(a) 
Agile:
    Pros - faster timeline, more resilient to change of scope
    Cons - Less time on each steps, need constant work
Non-Agile
    Pros: More time on each step, able ot create more finished product
    Cons - Slower timeline, less resilient to change of scope

(b) 
Product Owner – Sets direction for the team, handles releases
and stakeholder management
Scrum Master - All scrum related tasks – JIRA tooling, facilitating
sprints and meetings

(c)
Sprint Planning Meeting:
▪ Determine what to complete in the coming sprint
▪ Move stories in sprint scope from backlog to current sprint
▪ Determine story points and assign stories to team
▪ Start sprint
Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting:
▪ Short meeting that happens every day of the sprint
▪ Usually, 10 mins long
▪ Team members discuss work completed, work plan for the day and any blockers
Sprint Retrospective Meeting:
▪ Occurs at the end of the sprint
▪ Discuss work complete and move incomplete work to backlog
▪ Close sprint

(d)
Tasks
▪ Tasks are being used to plan specific tasks which should not take more than 1 working day.
Story
▪ Stories entail the most important project information: vision, goal, benefits, project team etc.
Epics
▪ An Epic could present the theme/topic to which a task belongs or represent a big project, for example “Migration customer X” in consulting or “Website Relaunch” in marketing.
Bug
▪ A bug is a problem which impairs or prevents the functions of a product.
